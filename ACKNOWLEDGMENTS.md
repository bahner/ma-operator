# Acknowledgments

## Background music

**"Impressions from the Alto Playroom at Xerox PARC"**

Composed and performed by **Chris Jeffers** on a Xerox Alto computer, produced at
Alan Kay's Learning Research Group, Xerox PARC.

The piece is in the public domain. An audio restoration is available at
<https://soundcloud.com/audimprove/happy-hacker>.
